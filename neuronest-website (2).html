Simple 4-step process

From concern to care — in 24 hours

Whether you're unsure where to start or already have a diagnosis, Autism Shakti guides you to the right support immediately.

1

Talk to our AI

Describe your child's situation. The AI guide helps you understand symptoms and points you to the right therapy type — no medical jargon.

2

Online consultation

Book a video session with a pediatric specialist who reviews your child's profile and recommends an intervention plan.

3

Choose your therapist

Browse certified therapists near you, read reviews, see availability, and pick the right fit for your child's needs and schedule.

4

Therapy at home

Your therapist arrives at your door. Sessions are logged, progress tracked, and shared with your consultation team after every visit.
